# Wizard Type 🧙

**Learn to type by casting spells!**

▶️ **Play here: https://dben.github.io/wizard-type/**

You are a little wizard with a very dusty tower. Every word you type is a magic spell.

- ✨ Type a word right and your wand shoots sparkles.
- 💨 Type a wrong letter and your spell goes *poof*. Try again!
- 🧹 Your spells sweep up dust, fix broken things, and help the village.
- 🐶 Cheer up grumpy animals. A bulldog, a goose, and even a dragon!
- ⭐ Earn up to 3 stars on every lesson.
- 🪙 Earn gold and buy hats, robes, wands, and things for your room.
- 🐱 Finish big lessons to get a pet friend.

## How to play

1. Make your wizard. Pick your hair and give them a name.
2. Put your fingers on the keyboard. Your pointer fingers go on **F** and **J**. Feel the little bumps!
3. Type the letters you see in the box.
4. The glowing key on the screen shows you what to press next.

**Tip:** Click the ✋ hand button to see where your fingers should go.

## For grown-ups

- Works in any modern browser on a computer with a keyboard.
- Progress is saved in the browser. Each kid can have their own wizard.
- To move a wizard to another computer, use **Grown-ups → Copy code** on the start screen.
- Made for ages 7 to 9. No accounts, no ads, nothing sent anywhere.
